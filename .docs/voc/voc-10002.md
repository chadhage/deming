# Know what to work on next

## Voice of the customer

"When my list gets long, I need to quickly see what is urgent and important so I can focus instead of feeling overwhelmed."

## User Story

As a person managing multiple responsibilities, I want to organize and filter my tasks by due date, priority, and status, so that I can confidently choose what to do next.

## Acceptance criteria

- I can assign or change a task's due date and priority.
- I can view tasks ordered by what is due soonest.
- I can filter the list to show active, completed, overdue, or high-priority tasks.
- My selected organization and filters produce a clear, predictable task list.

## Confirmations

- Overdue and high-priority tasks are easy to identify.
- Tasks without due dates or priorities remain accessible and are not lost.
